How payouts work

A coin launched with a fee split holds its fees in the coin's own vault until someone pays them out. Pay out sends each shareholder their share, so the platform share and yours move together.

Payouts land as SOL in the wallet that launched the coin. To move it, use Sweep on the Launch page, which links the destination to that wallet on-chain.

How it works

BurnerPad is a launcher, not a mixer. It keeps your main wallet out of the launch transaction. It does not hide where the money came from.

What happens

  1. Your browser creates a new keypair. The private key stays on your device and is never sent to BurnerPad.
  2. You send SOL to that address from a wallet or exchange you choose. This is your own transfer, signed by you.
  3. The burner signs the launch on the venue you pick. You can sweep leftover SOL back out afterwards.
  4. BurnerPad checks the launch on-chain and lists it. Coin pages show public checks: authorities, holders, launch-block buyers.

What it does not do

  • No pool, no custody. There is no deposit-and-withdraw pool. Nobody but you can move your funds.
  • Funding is visible. If you fund the burner straight from your main wallet, anyone can see the link on a block explorer. For real separation, fund it from an account that is not tied to you.
  • Sweeping links wallets. Sending leftovers or creator fees to your main wallet links the two on-chain. Keep them in the burner if separation matters.
  • Lose the key, lose the money. Back it up before you fund it. Clearing browser data can erase it.
